Hello Traveller…
It seems you have yet to be affected by this area’s curse. We’ve named it The Fading, as every sign of life and beauty becomes grey both in color and soul.
I see you have quite the impressive paintbrush on you, surely it is magical. That must be why you are unaffected by the curse.
Perhaps, you can stop this. Find the flowers of light, red, green, and blue. Once the essence of every flower has been captured by your paintbrush, you should be able to bring life back to the surrounding area. Just press ENTER to interact.
But beware, there are ghouls that roam these lands. People and creatures who have been completely taken by the curse, I’m not sure if they can ever come back from it…
It’s a lot of work, but I’m sure you can help restore this land to its former glory...
Let me give you a bit of advice.
The flowers of light in this area have allowed the forest to flourish. It is likely that they are each surrounded by life.
There are more lifeless regions to be helped. I will follow you to each of them and try my best to guide you. When life in this area has been restored, meet me at the teleportation portal North-East of here to progress to the next region.
Good luck Traveller…
